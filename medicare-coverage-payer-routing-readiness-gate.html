03

Readiness Logic

The gate is designed to block advancement when coverage is known but payer responsibility or claim destination is not.

Coverage detected / arrangement unknown

Identify the Medicare arrangement.

Do not infer payer responsibility from the word “Medicare” alone.

Plan identified / COB unresolved

Hold the workflow.

Correct plan identification does not close other-insurance or payer-order questions.

Primary payer unknown

Do not assign claim destination.

Establish the modeled payer-order status or route for qualified review.

Destination selected / evidence weak

Routing is not yet controlled.

Document evidence connecting the coverage arrangement and payer-order review to the selected destination.

Exception open / no owner

Advancement stays blocked.

Assign the next action and ownership before the case progresses.

All required controls verified

Ready to advance in simulation.

The modeled coverage, payer-order, service context, routing evidence, and closure controls are documented.
