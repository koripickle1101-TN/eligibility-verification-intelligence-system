04

Control design.

The project separates preventive, detective, corrective, and monitoring controls so the response is not reduced to “double-check the DOB.”

ControlTypePurpose in the simulation
Structured demographic read-backPreventiveAttempt to catch incorrect critical demographic information before eligibility.
Required-field validationPreventiveStop incomplete intake data from progressing.
Eligibility verificationDetectiveSurface an exception when submitted information needs investigation.
Exception gateOperationalPrevent unresolved exceptions from moving silently into later workflow steps.
Correction + reverificationCorrectiveResolve validated data issues and confirm the workflow can continue.
Root-cause classificationMonitoringTrace the visible exception back to the earliest controllable failure point.
05

Method & assumptions.

This is a deliberately bounded student experiment designed to demonstrate workflow reasoning—not clinical, payer, coding, compliance, or production-system authority.

Population30 synthetic cases

15 baseline and 15 control-state cases.

Defect mix12 modeled defects

DOB, member ID, subscriber, missing-field, and payer-selection scenarios.

Control assumption5 of 6 defects caught early

The control-state capture rate is intentionally modeled for comparison and is not a real benchmark.

Outcome boundaryNo real performance claim

Rates, minutes, and rework touches are synthetic design values.

Career insight.

A workflow exception is not only something to fix. It can be evidence that an earlier control failed—or that the control was missing.
