Healthcare Front-End Revenue Cycle Intelligence

Eligibility Verification Intelligence System

A student-developed healthcare operations portfolio project for exploring how insurance eligibility risk can be detected before scheduling, authorization, claim submission, or patient billing are affected.

Built by Kori Pickle to demonstrate student-level eligibility workflow analysis, patient access risk detection, clean intake readiness, and no-PHI healthcare operations documentation.

View New Control SimulationView Dashboard

No PHI / Synthetic Data Notice

All data shown in this project is synthetic and created for portfolio demonstration purposes. No patient information, protected health information, employer data, payer data, claims data, or real eligibility transactions are used.

Core Front-End Revenue Cycle Insight

Eligibility exceptions can be downstream signals of earlier intake defects. The operational question is not only what failed—but where the workflow first lost control.

Recruiter Quick Read

A 60-second overview of who built this, what it studies, and what the portfolio demonstrates.

Who built this?

Kori Pickle, BSHA Candidate focused on healthcare operations, patient access, revenue cycle workflow analysis, and denial prevention.

What problem does it study?

EVIS examines how eligibility and intake defects can be identified before they disrupt later workflow steps.

What skills does it demonstrate?

Eligibility workflow analysis, clean intake readiness, KPI design, risk scoring, synthetic data design, and remote-ready documentation.

What roles does it support?

Entry-level patient access, eligibility verification, prior authorization support, revenue cycle support, and healthcare operations opportunities.
